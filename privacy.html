Privacy Policy

App: Matzscho TikTok Composio Connector

Last updated: July 24, 2026

Overview

This app is a private TikTok OAuth connector used by the owner to connect their own TikTok account to Composio/Hermes for personal automation.

Data accessed

With explicit TikTok OAuth authorization, the app may access basic TikTok account/profile information and profile statistics, depending on the scopes granted. This may include username, profile information, and public/account statistics made available by TikTok APIs.

Purpose of processing

The data is used only to display or process the owner's TikTok profile information and statistics inside the owner's personal Composio/Hermes automation environment.

Data sharing

The app does not sell personal data and does not provide data to advertisers or data brokers. Data may be technically processed by TikTok, Composio, Hermes, and infrastructure providers as required to perform OAuth authentication and API calls.

Storage

This app does not operate a separate public user database. OAuth tokens and connection metadata, if created, are handled by Composio and related infrastructure used for the integration.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked by disconnecting the TikTok account in Composio and/or removing app authorization in TikTok account settings.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the owner via github.com/matzscho.
